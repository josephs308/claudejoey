Experience on both sides of the courtroom.

McMillan & Black is a Texas law firm of trial lawyers who have represented plaintiffs and defendants, in civil and criminal matters, in state and federal court, at trial and on appeal. That range of experience means we understand how the other side builds its case, and how to take it apart.

Our clients are homeowners, property owners, builders, business owners and families settling an estate. What they have in common is a dispute over something that matters: a home, a business, a contract or an inheritance. We take those matters from the first demand letter through trial when that is what it takes.

Every new matter starts with a free, same-day consultation, so you know where you stand before you commit to anything.

How we can help

Real Estate Law

Title and boundary disputes, failed closings, landlord and tenant conflicts, and real estate transactions.

Construction Law

Construction defects, contractor disputes and mechanic's lien claims for owners, builders and subcontractors.

Consumer Protection

Claims under the Texas Deceptive Trade Practices Act, including cases against residential solar companies.

Business Law

Partnership and contract disputes, and the agreements that keep a business protected.

Probate & Estates

Estate administration and contested probate, including disputes between heirs.

HOA & Property Owners Associations

Litigation & Appeals

Civil trial and appellate work in Texas state and federal courts.

Free Speech Litigation

Defamation claims and defenses, and motions under the Texas Citizens Participation Act.
Solar Panel Fraud

Sold solar panels that never delivered what you were promised?

Many Texas homeowners were told their panels would pay for themselves, eliminate their electric bill or qualify for tax credits that never materialized. Now they're left with a loan and a system that doesn't perform.

We represent homeowners against residential solar companies under the Texas Deceptive Trade Practices Act. If you were misled, you may be entitled to recover what you've paid and more.
