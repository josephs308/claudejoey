Meet your attorney

Tyler Thompson

Founder & trial attorney. Twelve years inside the insurance industry. Now in your corner — using everything he learned there to make them pay what you're owed.

Tyler Thompson founded Thompson Law STL in 2022 for one reason: to give injured people the same caliber of advocate that insurance companies have always had. He knows that caliber well — because for more than a decade, he was that advocate for the other side.

From defending insurers to fighting them

Tyler began his career the way many strong trial lawyers do — clerking at large law firms during law school, then stepping into the defense side of personal injury litigation. For eight years, he defended the clients of a large St. Louis firm, including insurance carriers and the companies they protect. He then spent four years in-house at a national insurance company, sitting at the very center of how claims are evaluated, negotiated, and paid.

That path gave him something most plaintiff's attorneys never get: a complete, first-hand understanding of how the insurance machine actually works. He has read the internal guidelines. He knows how adjusters are trained to value — and quietly devalue — a claim, which injuries they take seriously, and exactly where they look to cut what they pay.

Eventually, Tyler realized the people who needed that knowledge most weren't the insurers. They were the injured people on the other side of the file. So he switched sides for good.

The advantage you get

When Tyler takes your case, he's not guessing at the insurance company's next move — he's already seen it. He knows what a claim is really worth, when an offer is a lowball, and what it takes to make a carrier take you seriously. And because he built cases for the defense, he builds yours to withstand everything the defense will throw at it.

His background spans insurance defense, personal injury, product liability, and property-damage litigation. Over 16+ years he has handled hundreds of cases through settlement and trial, and since founding the firm he has recovered more than $7 million for his clients.

How Tyler works with clients

  • You work directly with Tyler. Not a paralegal, not a rotating "case manager" — the attorney on your case is the person who answers when you call.
  • Straight answers. He'll tell you honestly where your case stands, even when it isn't what you hoped to hear.
  • Trial-ready from day one. Every case is prepared as if it's going to a jury, because that's what pushes insurers to pay full value.
  • No fee unless he wins. You never pay out of pocket. The fee comes from the recovery — and only if there is one.
