# Vincere Legal Marketing: cold emails for the 9 channels

One email per channel, plus a multi-channel opener and a follow-up sequence.
Each one is built on a play with a track record in agency cold outreach:

- **Show a result you found about them.** Something they can check in 10 seconds.
- **Keep it short.** Under 90 words, plain text, no links or images in email 1.
- **Ask a question they can answer in one word.** Ask for interest, not a meeting.
- **Offer something free and specific.** For Vincere that's the free written plan
  and the approval-first website preview, both already on the site.

Variables: `{{first_name}}`, `{{firm}}`, `{{city}}`, `{{practice}}` (e.g. "car accident"),
`{{competitor}}`, `{{sender}}`. Anything in [brackets] is a finding you have to look up
by hand before sending. **Never send one with the bracket still in it.**

Do not invent client results. Until you have real case studies, the finding about
their own firm is your proof.

## Follow-up sequence (for every angle)

**Follow-up 1 (3 days later, same thread):**
> {{first_name}}, putting this back at the top of your inbox. Want me to send it over?

**Follow-up 2 (4 days later, same thread): add value, don't just nudge:**
> One more thing I noticed: [a second finding from a different channel, e.g.
> "your Google profile has 4 unanswered reviews from this month"]. The free plan
> covers both. Want it?

**Follow-up 3 (7 days later, the break-up):**
> I'll stop reaching out after this one. If client acquisition isn't a priority for
> {{firm}} right now, no worries. If it is, reply "plan" and I'll send yours over.

---

## Running these across the sending inboxes

- **One angle per inbox for the first 2 weeks.** Then you'll know which angle
  gets replies instead of guessing.
- **Match the angle to the firm.** PI and criminal defense: LSA, PPC, speed-to-lead.
  Estate, family and bankruptcy: Meta, SEO. Firms with billboards: Traditional.
  Slow site: the Website preview.
- **Volume:** stay under about 30 to 40 sends per inbox per day, and keep warmup running.
- **Compliance:** include a physical address and an opt-out line
  ("Not relevant? Reply 'no' and I won't follow up"). That's required under CAN-SPAM.
- **Measure positive replies per 100 sends,** not opens. Open tracking is unreliable and hurts deliverability.

# Insider openers: whole-agency pitch

Vincere has no attorney, so nothing here claims to be one. Don't name any past
company or client. The credibility comes from having worked inside law firm
marketing, talking about a problem every managing partner recognizes.

costs.

I started Vincere to be the one team that runs all of it and answers that
question in one dashboard.

Can you tell me what a signed case cost {{firm}} last month? If not, I'd like to
show you how we'd find out.

Joey

SEO, referrals, Facebook or something else?

Joey, Vincere Legal Marketing

## How to use them

- **Sequence:** send #4 or #1 first, then #3 as the follow-up, so the free plan
