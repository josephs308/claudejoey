More people now ask AI tools who to hire before they ever see a list of search results. Someone might ask ChatGPT for a good divorce lawyer near them, or read a Google AI Overview that summarizes their options. If your firm is not part of those answers, you can lose the client before they start comparing websites.

Answer engine optimization, or AEO, is how you become part of those answers.

## What AEO means

AEO is the work of making your firm easy for AI tools to find, understand and trust. These tools pull information from websites, business profiles, reviews and other sources, then write a direct answer. AEO makes sure the information they find about you is clear, accurate and quotable.

![A computer screen showing Google search results](photo-1586125674857-4eb86880905d "Classic search results and AI answers now sit side by side.")

## How AEO is different from SEO

Traditional SEO focuses on ranking your pages in the list of search results. AEO focuses on being named inside the answer itself. The two work together, but AEO puts extra weight on a few things:

- **Direct answers.** Pages that answer a question in the first sentence are easier for AI tools to quote.
- **Structured data.** Schema markup tells machines exactly who you are, what you do and where you work.
- **Consistency.** Your firm name, address, phone number and practice areas should match everywhere they appear online.
- **Trusted mentions.** Reviews, directory listings and coverage on reputable sites help AI tools decide you are a safe recommendation.

## Where a law firm should start with AEO

1. **List the questions clients ask.** Think about what people ask before they hire a lawyer in your practice area, in plain language.
2. **Answer them directly on your site.** Give each important question a clear answer at the top of a relevant page, then explain in more detail below.
3. **Add structured data.** Mark up your organization, services, FAQs and pages so the details are unambiguous.
4. **Clean up your business details.** Make your Google Business Profile, directories and website agree on the same facts.
5. **Track where you appear.** Check which AI answers mention your firm for your key questions, and watch how that changes.

## What AEO does not replace

AEO does not replace good intake, a fast website or a strong reputation. AI tools recommend firms that look credible from every angle. If a prospect does find you through an AI answer, your website and your team still have to turn that interest into a signed case.
