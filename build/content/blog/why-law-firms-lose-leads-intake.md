When marketing stops producing clients, most firms look at the ads first. Often the problem is somewhere else: the leads are arriving, and the firm is losing them between the first ring and the signed agreement. Every lost lead is one you already paid for.

## Where law firm leads leak

### Calls that go unanswered

Calls at lunch, after hours and on weekends often go to voicemail. Many people looking for a lawyer do not leave a message. They call the next firm on the list.

### Slow replies to web forms and messages

A form submitted at 9 p.m. and answered the next afternoon is competing with firms that replied in minutes. By then the person may have already booked somewhere else.

### One attempt and done

A missed callback or an unanswered email is not a no. People are busy and stressed. Firms that follow up several times, across calls, texts and email, reach people that one-attempt firms never do.

### No clear next step

If a call ends with "someone will get back to you", the lead is still up for grabs. The strongest intake ends every first conversation with a booked consultation.

### No tracking

If you cannot see which leads came in, who handled them and what happened next, you cannot see the leaks at all.

## How to fix intake before spending more

1. **Answer every inquiry, every hour.** Use staff, an answering service or AI intake, as long as each one can screen and book.
2. **Reply to web leads in minutes.** Send an instant acknowledgment, then a real response quickly.
3. **Ask the same screening questions every time.** Practice area, location, timing and conflicts, so the right cases move forward quickly.
4. **Book the consultation on the first contact.** Offer specific times instead of promising a callback.
5. **Follow up on a schedule.** Several attempts over several days, across more than one channel.
6. **Track every lead to an outcome.** Booked, signed, not a fit or lost, so you can see where the leaks are.

> More leads will not fix a firm that loses the leads it already has.

## Why this comes before more advertising

Advertising multiplies whatever intake you already have. If intake loses half your leads, doubling your ad spend doubles the waste. Fixing intake first makes every marketing dollar after it work harder, and it shows up directly in a lower cost per signed case.
