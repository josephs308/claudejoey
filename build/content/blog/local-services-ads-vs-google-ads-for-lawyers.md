Local Services Ads and Google Search Ads both put a law firm at the top of Google, but they work in very different ways. One charges you for leads and decides most of the details for you. The other charges you for clicks and lets you control nearly everything. Which one fits depends on your practice area, your market and how much control you want.

## How Local Services Ads work

Local Services Ads appear at the very top of many local legal searches, such as "divorce lawyer near me". Each ad shows the firm's name, its Google reviews and, once verified, the Google Screened badge.

- **You pay per lead**, not per click. A lead is usually a phone call or a message through Google.
- **You choose practice areas and service area**, and set a budget. You do not choose keywords or write ad copy.
- **Ranking leans on reviews and responsiveness.** Firms with strong reviews that answer their phones tend to show more often.
- **Poor-fit leads can be disputed.** If a call is about a service you do not offer, you can often ask Google for a credit.

The trade-off is control. Google decides which searches trigger your ad and how it looks.

## How Google Search Ads work

Google Search Ads, often called PPC, appear in the ad slots on the results page. You bid on the searches you want and pay each time someone clicks.

- **You choose the keywords**, including the ones you never want to show for.
- **You write the ads** and pick the landing page each one sends people to.
- **You pay per click**, so the cost of a lead depends on both click prices and how well your landing page turns visitors into calls and forms.
- **You can target narrowly**, by location, time of day, device and audience.

The trade-off is effort. Search Ads reward careful keyword work, strong landing pages and constant tuning, and they can waste money quickly without them.

## Side by side

- **What you pay for:** Local Services Ads charge per lead. Search Ads charge per click.
- **Control:** Local Services Ads give you little. Search Ads give you nearly all of it.
- **Setup:** Local Services Ads need the Google Screened verification first. Search Ads need keywords, ads and landing pages.
- **What drives results:** for Local Services Ads, reviews and answering every call. For Search Ads, keyword choice, ad quality and landing-page conversion.

## When to start with Local Services Ads

Local Services Ads often make sense first for a firm that has solid Google reviews, can answer every call quickly and works in a practice area people search for locally. The pay-per-lead model also limits waste while you learn what a client costs in your market.

## When to start with Google Search Ads

Search Ads often make sense first when you need control: you want specific case types, you want to avoid certain searches, or your practice area is not well covered by Local Services Ads. They also suit firms that already have fast, focused landing pages.

## Why most firms end up running both

The two channels show up in different places and reach people at slightly different moments, so they rarely cannibalize each other completely. The honest way to decide how to split the budget is to track each channel to signed cases, not clicks or leads, and move money toward whichever signs clients at the lower cost.

> Answering the phone matters as much as the ad. A lead you pay for and miss is the most expensive lead you will ever buy.
