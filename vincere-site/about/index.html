About Vincere Legal Marketing

Vincere Legal Marketing is a full-service marketing partner for US law firms. We connect the right specialist teams for each channel and keep them working from one plan and one set of numbers. Your firm deals with one point of contact, and every dollar is judged by the cases it signs.

Get my free plan Call (555) 010-0199

One partner instead of a stack of vendors

Many firms end up with an SEO vendor, a separate ads agency, a web developer and a billboard rep, each reporting different numbers and none responsible for signed cases. Managing partners spend hours reconciling reports and still cannot say which channel is working.

Vincere replaces that with one relationship. We bring in the specialist teams each channel needs, from search and paid media to production and web development, and coordinate them against a single plan. You get one point of contact, one dashboard and one answer to the question of where the next dollar should go.

A plan before you pay

Every engagement starts with a free plan. We learn your practice areas, case values, intake and close rate, and capacity. Then we price your market: click costs, cost per lead, competitor spend and how crowded the map pack and Local Services Ads are.

From that we recommend a channel mix, budget split, launch order and expected cost per case. There is no obligation and no retainer to start. If the numbers do not work in your market, we say so, because a plan that looks good on paper and loses money helps nobody.

Intake first, then measured in signed cases

Before we add new leads, we look at what happens to the ones you already get: how fast calls are answered, how follow-up works and how consultations get booked. Fixing speed to lead and follow-up often turns more existing leads into signed cases without spending more.

Then everything is tracked through to outcome. Your client dashboard shows spend by channel, calls, consultations and cost per signed case, live and in one place. We report on signed cases rather than clicks or impressions, because that is the number that pays your firm’s bills.
