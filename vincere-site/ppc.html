Where businesses lose the money

Broad match with no negative discipline, a single campaign covering six offers with wildly different values, landing traffic on a homepage, and no conversion signal past the form fill. Each one is common. Together they can double your cost per customer.

Feeding the algorithm the right signal

Google optimizes toward whatever you tell it success is. If success is a form fill, you get cheap form fills from people who will never buy. We push closed-sale data back into the account through offline conversion import, so the bidding learns to chase sales instead of clicks.

What’s included

  • Account restructured by product/service and value, not lumped into one campaign
  • Aggressive negative keyword discipline, reviewed weekly against search terms
  • Geo-targeting and dayparting matched to when your team is actually available to respond
  • Dedicated landing pages per offer, not homepage traffic
  • Call tracking with recording, plus form and chat attribution
  • Offline conversion import so bidding optimizes toward new customers
  • Competitor conquesting and brand defence where the math supports it
  • Budget pacing and monthly reporting tied to cost per new customer
