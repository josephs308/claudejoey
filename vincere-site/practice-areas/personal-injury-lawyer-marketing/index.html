Quick answer

How do personal injury lawyers get more clients?

Personal injury lawyers get more clients by showing up where injured people search first, usually Google Local Services Ads, the map pack and paid search, then answering every call within minutes. Firms that also build organic authority, fix intake follow-up and track cost per signed case, not cost per lead, tend to grow most predictably.

What makes personal injury marketing different.

Get a plan for your market →
  1. 01

    The most expensive clicks in legal

    Car accident and truck accident keywords are among the priciest in Google Ads, and large firms bid on them all day. A small or mid-size PI firm has to pick its fights by case type, geography and time of day.

  2. 02

    The first firm to answer usually wins

    An injured person often calls several firms from the same search results page. The one that picks up, qualifies quickly and books a sign-up meeting tends to get the retainer.

  3. 03

    Case value varies wildly

    A soft tissue MVA, a commercial truck crash and a premises liability fall carry very different fee potential. Marketing that treats every lead the same overpays for low-value cases and underinvests in the ones that fund the firm.

  4. 04

    Advertising rules are strict here

    PI advertising draws scrutiny under state bar rules and ABA Model Rule 7.1, especially around results, testimonials and solicitation timing. Copy and ads need to be written with those limits in mind from the first draft.

Personal injury lawyer marketing starts with the math

Before any spend goes live, Vincere builds a free plan from your own numbers. We look at your average fee by case type, your current sign rate, how many new files your team can actually work, and your settlement timeline. Then we price your market: cost per click on MVA and truck terms, what LSA leads run in your metro, how crowded the map pack is, and who is spending on TV and billboards. If the numbers do not support a profitable launch in your area, we tell you before you commit a retainer.

The plan ends with a channel mix, a budget split and a launch order. Most PI firms do not need every channel on day one. A newer firm might start with Local Services Ads and tight Google Search campaigns on its best case types, while an established firm with a strong review base might lean harder into SEO and answer engine visibility. Each recommendation comes with an expected cost per signed case, so you can judge it like any other business investment.

Single event cases, not mass tort chasing

Most of our PI work focuses on single event cases: motor vehicle accidents, truck crashes, motorcycle and pedestrian injuries, dog bites, slip and falls and other premises liability claims. These cases come from local, urgent searches, so proximity, reviews and responsiveness matter more than national reach. We build campaigns and landing pages around each case type, with separate budgets, so a flood of low-value fender benders does not eat the money meant for serious injury calls.

Mass tort acquisition is a different model with different economics, often involving co-counsel arrangements, national media buys and large lead volumes. If your firm participates in mass torts, we will tell you honestly whether that belongs in the same plan or should be handled separately. For most local PI practices, the best return comes from owning your metro for the cases you already sign well, then expanding into adjacent counties or case types once intake and cost per case are stable.

Personal injury intake: minutes decide the case

An injured caller is often in pain, dealing with insurers and dialing several firms. If your line goes to voicemail, the next firm signs the case. We map every lead path, from LSA calls to web forms and chats, and set response targets measured in minutes, including nights and weekends. We build qualification scripts around liability, injuries, treatment and insurance, then set follow-up sequences for callers who do not sign on the first call. E-sign retainers and same-day sign-up visits close the gap between a good call and a signed contingency agreement.
