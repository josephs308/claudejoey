Employment Lawyer Marketing That Finds Cases Worth Taking

Quick answer

How do employment lawyers get more clients?

Employment lawyers get more clients by ranking and advertising for specific claims like wrongful termination, discrimination, retaliation and unpaid overtime, then screening inquiries quickly for merit, damages and deadlines. Clear content on workers’ rights, Local Services Ads, targeted paid search and fast intake turn a high volume of workplace complaints into signed cases.

The market

What makes employment law marketing different.

Many calls, fewer viable claims

People often call after being fired or mistreated, but not every bad workplace experience is a legal claim. Screening efficiency matters as much as lead volume.

Deadlines drive urgency

Agency charge deadlines, such as those for filing with the EEOC or a state agency, can be short. Prospects need to know that waiting can cost them their claim.

Case value depends on facts

Wage and hour class claims, executive severance negotiations and single-plaintiff discrimination cases have very different economics. Campaigns should reflect the mix your firm wants.

Employee side or employer side

Plaintiff firms and management-side firms reach very different audiences. Marketing for employers looks more like business law marketing, with a longer sales cycle.

Employment lawyer marketing by claim type

A search for "wrongful termination lawyer" and a search for "unpaid overtime attorney" come from different people with different case values. Vincere builds separate campaigns and pages for each claim type your firm handles: discrimination, sexual harassment, retaliation, whistleblower claims, FMLA and disability accommodation, wage and hour, and severance negotiation. That lets budget flow to the cases that fit your fee model, whether contingency, hourly or hybrid.

Your free plan prices each claim type in your market, looks at LSA and map pack competition, and reviews which of your current cases came from which sources. We then recommend a channel mix and launch order with an expected cost per signed case. If a claim type is saturated with large advertisers in your metro, we will say so and point budget where your firm can compete.

Content that educates and screens at the same time

Workers often search questions before they search for a lawyer: whether they can be fired for complaining, whether a salaried employee gets overtime, how long they have to file a discrimination charge. Clear, careful answers to those questions build organic traffic and get cited by AI assistants. They also help prospects self-screen, so the people who call are more likely to have a claim your firm can pursue.

Content must be general information, not legal advice, and it should be accurate for your state. We write with state bar advertising rules in mind and route pages to your attorneys for review. The result is a library of pages that rank for employment questions, support paid campaigns with strong landing pages, and reduce the number of unproductive consultations your team sits through.

One partner, one set of numbers

Employment firms often have lead sources scattered across LSA, directory listings, a website, co-counsel referrals and occasional paid search. Vincere consolidates that into one plan. As your single marketing partner, we connect the right specialist teams for each channel and keep them working from one budget and one set of numbers. You have one point of contact, and we manage the coordination.

Your client dashboard shows spend by channel, calls, screened intakes, consultations and cost per signed case, live. For employment firms, a useful view is signed cases by claim type and source, which quickly shows whether wage claims, discrimination cases or severance matters are producing the best return. That makes budget decisions straightforward and grounded in cases, not clicks, and it shows where intake screening needs tightening.

Intake

Employment intake: screen fast, respect deadlines

Employment firms receive many calls from people who were treated unfairly but may not have a legal claim. Intake needs to sort them quickly and kindly. We build scripts and online questionnaires covering employer size, dates of employment and termination, protected activity or characteristics, pay issues and any agency charges already filed. Promising inquiries get a consultation within a day or two, with deadline risks flagged to an attorney immediately. Declined prospects receive a polite, clear response and, where appropriate, a referral resource, which protects your reviews and reputation.
