Medical Malpractice

Medical Malpractice Lawyer Marketing for Viable, High-Value Claims

Medical malpractice firms do not need more calls. They need more viable claims and a faster way to screen out the rest. Quick answer

How do medical malpractice lawyers get more cases?

Medical malpractice lawyers get more cases by publishing authoritative content on specific injuries like birth injuries, surgical errors and misdiagnosis, running search ads narrowly targeted to serious harm, and building referral relationships with other firms. Rigorous intake screening for causation, damages and deadlines matters as much as lead volume, because few inquiries become viable claims.

The market

What makes medical malpractice marketing different.

Most inquiries are not viable

Many callers had a bad outcome but not a provable deviation from the standard of care, or their damages cannot justify expert costs. Marketing and intake must attract serious cases and screen efficiently.

Case costs are high before a dime is recovered

Expert reviews, records and certificate of merit requirements in many states mean each accepted case is a major investment. Acquisition spend has to be judged against realistic case value and win probability.

Research cycles are long

Families often spend weeks reading about a diagnosis, a birth injury or a surgical complication before contacting a lawyer. Educational content that answers medical and legal questions earns trust across that long window.

Referrals drive a large share of cases

General PI firms and other lawyers frequently refer malpractice matters to specialists. Strong branding and a reputation for handling complex claims keep those referrals coming.

Medical malpractice lawyer marketing focused on serious harm

Broad terms like ’malpractice lawyer’ attract a wide mix of inquiries, many about billing disputes, rude staff or minor complications. Our medical malpractice lawyer marketing leans into specific, serious injury categories: birth injuries such as hypoxic brain injury and brachial plexus injuries, delayed cancer diagnosis, surgical errors, anesthesia errors, medication errors, and wrongful death after treatment. Pages, ad groups and FAQs built around those categories attract people whose situations are more likely to support a claim.

Paid search needs the same discipline. We use exact and phrase match on serious injury terms, long negative keyword lists, and landing pages that explain in plain language what makes a case viable, including the role of expert review and statutes of limitations. That content filters itself, because readers with minor issues self-select out, while families facing lifelong harm see a firm that understands their situation and is worth calling.

Building the credibility these families look for

A family considering a lawsuit against a hospital or physician wants to know your firm has handled complex medical cases and has the resources to see one through. Your website should show attorney backgrounds, medical knowledge, how you work with experts, and how contingency fees and case costs work. We build detailed practice pages for each injury type and write them to be medically accurate, compassionate and specific to how claims work in your state.

Authority content also matters for AI search. People now ask ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity questions like ’was my baby’s brain injury preventable’ or ’how long do I have to sue for misdiagnosis’. Clear, well-structured answers, reviewed by your attorneys, help your firm get cited in those answers. We follow ABA Model Rule 7.1 and your state bar’s advertising rules, so nothing implies an outcome your firm cannot promise.

Screening intake so attorneys spend time on the right cases

Medical malpractice intake is a filtering job. A good screen captures the provider and facility, dates of treatment, what happened, the injury and its permanence, and when the family first suspected a problem. It flags statute of limitations and notice deadlines, and it identifies government facility claims that may have short notice periods. We build intake scripts and CRM workflows that route promising inquiries to an attorney quickly and send respectful declination letters to the rest.

Our free plan starts with your case criteria, average case value, accept rate and capacity, then prices your market so you know the expected cost per signed case for each channel. For many malpractice firms, the right mix is SEO and AEO for authority, focused search ads for high-intent terms, and referral branding. Your client dashboard shows spend, inquiries, qualified inquiries and signed cases by source, so you can see which channels bring viable claims.

Intake

Intake built to qualify, not just collect

Medical malpractice intake should quickly gather the provider, facility, dates, injury, permanence and how the family learned something went wrong, then flag limitation and notice deadlines. Promising inquiries should reach an attorney or nurse reviewer within a day, with records authorization forms sent right away. Inquiries that do not meet your criteria deserve a prompt, respectful declination that notes deadlines may apply. We build these workflows into your CRM and report qualified inquiry rate and signed cases by source, so marketing spend follows the channels that produce viable claims.
