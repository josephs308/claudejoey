Quick answer

How is marketing different for each practice area?

Marketing differs by practice area because urgency, case value, fee structure and client behavior differ. Criminal defense and DUI need 24/7 intake and fast local search, personal injury depends on case value and competition, estate planning relies on trust and referrals, and IP or business law often competes nationally. The channel mix should follow those realities.
