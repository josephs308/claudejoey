Quick answer

How do criminal defense lawyers get more clients?

Criminal defense lawyers get more clients by being visible the moment someone is arrested or charged, usually through Google Local Services Ads, paid search and the map pack, and by answering around the clock. Charge-specific pages, strong reviews and fast consultation booking turn those urgent searches into paid retainers.

The market

What makes criminal defense marketing different.

Urgency runs the whole funnel

Most defense searches happen within hours or days of an arrest, often late at night or on weekends. A firm that only answers during business hours loses a large share of its best leads.

The caller is often not the client

Parents, spouses and friends frequently make the first call while the accused is in custody. Messaging and intake have to speak to a worried family member as well as the defendant.

Charge type drives case value

A first-offense misdemeanor, a felony drug charge and a federal white collar matter differ enormously in fee and complexity. Campaigns should separate them so budget follows the cases your firm wants.

Privacy shapes behavior

People facing charges rarely ask friends for a referral and often search privately. Reviews, clear fee conversations and a discreet first contact carry unusual weight.

Criminal defense lawyer marketing built around charge types

A generic "criminal lawyer" campaign mixes shoplifting, domestic violence, drug possession, theft and felony assault into one budget, and the cheapest clicks usually win. Vincere splits your marketing by charge type and court, so a DUI search, a probation violation search and a federal indictment search each land on a page written for that situation. That lets us fund the matters your firm prices well and wants more of, and pull back on low-fee charges that fill the calendar without moving revenue.

Geography matters just as much. Defense clients search by county and courthouse, and your ability to appear in a specific jurisdiction is a real selling point. We build location pages around the courts you actually practice in, not every city in the state. Your free plan shows local click costs, LSA competition and how crowded the map pack is for each charge type, then recommends where to launch first and what a retained case should cost.

Winning the late-night search

Arrests do not follow office hours. Paid search schedules, LSA availability settings and call routing all need to match when your prospects actually search. We set ad schedules so budget is live when bookings spike, route calls to a person rather than a voicemail box, and give after-hours callers a clear next step. For many defense firms, simply answering between 9 p.m. and 7 a.m. changes results more than any new ad channel.

Families searching late at night also want quick answers: how bail works, whether they can visit, what happens at arraignment. Clear, plainly written pages on those questions help your firm rank in search and get named in AI answers from ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews. They also build trust before the first call, which matters when someone is choosing a lawyer in a stressful moment and has never hired one before.

One partner, measured by retainers

Vincere is your single marketing partner. We connect the specialist teams for LSA, PPC, SEO, your website and any traditional media, and keep them on one plan and one set of numbers. You deal with one point of contact instead of juggling vendors who each claim credit for the same call. We handle coordination, reporting and the hard conversations about which channels are not pulling their weight.

Your client dashboard tracks spend by channel, calls, consultations booked and cost per retained case in real time. Defense firms often find that one charge type or one county produces most of their profitable retainers while another burns money on calls that never pay a fee. Seeing that in one place lets you shift budget quickly, which matters in a practice where demand can swing with local enforcement patterns.

Intake

Criminal defense intake that never sleeps

Defense leads decay fast. A family that cannot reach you tonight will hire whoever answers. We set up true 24/7 coverage, whether that is your own on-call rotation or a trained answering service with clear escalation rules. Scripts cover the charge, custody status, next court date and who is paying, without giving legal advice. Follow-up happens by text and call within minutes, consult slots are bookable online, and fee agreements can be signed electronically. We also set rules for jail calls and collect callbacks so none are lost overnight.
