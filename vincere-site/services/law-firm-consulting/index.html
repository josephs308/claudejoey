Quick answer

What is law firm consulting?

Law firm consulting is a hands-on review and rebuild of how a firm turns inquiries into signed clients. It covers intake scripts, response time, CRM setup, follow-up automations, consult booking and reporting, so every lead is tracked from first call to signed engagement and marketing spend is judged by cost per signed case.

Why law firm consulting starts with intake, not ads

Buying more leads for a firm that answers slowly is like pouring water into a cracked bucket. A potential client who calls after a crash, an arrest or a termination usually contacts more than one firm, and the first attorney who calls back with a clear next step often wins the case. Before we recommend any channel, we look at how fast your team responds, what happens after hours, and how many callers never get a second touch.

That review usually surfaces problems a managing partner cannot see from the P&L. Voicemails that sit until Monday. Web forms routed to a shared inbox nobody owns. Consults booked but never confirmed, so no-shows pile up. Fixing these gaps costs far less than new advertising, and it makes every channel you add later perform better, because the leads you already pay for stop leaking out of the funnel.

CRM, automations and lead-to-client workflows

We map the path a lead takes from first contact to signed retainer, then build it into your CRM so nothing depends on memory. That means lead sources tagged correctly, intake stages that match how your firm actually works, and automated text and email follow-up that fires when a caller does not book. We work with the intake and practice management tools legal teams commonly use, and we help you choose one if your current setup is a spreadsheet.

Automation should support your people, not replace the human conversation a scared client needs. We write follow-up sequences in plain language, keep them compliant with the attorney advertising rules in your state, and set clear handoffs to a live intake specialist or attorney. Your team gets task reminders, missed-call alerts and a daily view of which leads are waiting, so the right person calls back while the prospect still wants to talk.

Client management and reporting you can run the firm on

Signing the case is not the end of the job. Clients who hear nothing for weeks leave bad reviews, fire the firm or never refer a friend. We help you set up status updates, review requests at the right moments and referral follow-up, so satisfied clients become a steady source of new matters instead of a missed opportunity. These workflows are simple to run and easy for a small staff to keep up.

Every part of the process feeds your Vincere client dashboard. You see calls, consultations, signed cases and cost per signed case by channel, live, in one place. When intake data and marketing data finally sit side by side, budget decisions get easier. You can tell which sources produce clients rather than tire kickers, and you can hold every vendor, including us, to the same set of numbers.
