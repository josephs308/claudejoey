What marketing services do law firms need?

Most law firms need a working intake process first, then a mix of channels matched to their practice and market: SEO and AEO for organic and AI search, Google Local Services Ads and PPC for immediate demand, Meta ads and traditional media for awareness, plus a fast website and clear branding.
