Answers

Personal injury questions, answered straight.

The questions people actually ask us — with direct answers, no legalese.

Nothing up front. Thompson Law STL works on a contingency fee — there's no cost to start and no fee at all unless we recover money for you. The consultation is free, and our fee is simply a percentage of what we win. If there's no recovery, you owe us nothing.
In Missouri, the deadline for most personal injury claims is five years from the date of injury (two years for medical malpractice). In Illinois it's generally two years. Those deadlines are strict and evidence disappears quickly, so the sooner you call, the stronger your case.
Be very careful. Adjusters are trained to minimize payouts, and a recorded statement can be twisted against you. Tyler spent years defending insurance companies, so he knows the script. Talk to a lawyer before giving a statement or accepting any early offer — it's free to ask.
It depends on your medical bills, lost income, the severity and permanence of your injuries, pain and suffering, and the insurance coverage available. Anyone who quotes a number before reviewing the facts is guessing. We'll give you an honest assessment in a free case review.
Most cases settle without a trial. But insurers pay more when they know your attorney is truly ready to try the case. We prepare every case for trial from day one — which is often exactly what produces a better settlement.
