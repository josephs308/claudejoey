Why this case matters

What actually decides the value of this claim.

Spinal cord damage, traumatic brain injury, amputation, severe burns. These injuries do not only change a body — they change what work is possible, what a home has to be, who provides care and for how long. Compensation has to be built across a full life expectancy, because that is the span over which the cost is actually incurred.

Our approach on these files is quantitative: complete medical documentation, a certified life-care plan, and a forensic economist who reduces the whole projection to present value. A number arrived at that way is defensible in front of a jury, which is precisely why carriers negotiate against it seriously.

What you need to know

Read these six things before you talk to an adjuster.

Each one has changed the value of a real file. Most claimants learn them too late to use.

Certified life-care planLifetime care must be costed, not estimated

A credentialed planner projects every future surgery, medication, assistive device, home modification, vehicle adaptation and hour of attendant care across your life expectancy, adjusted for medical inflation.

Forensic economicsLost earning capacity is not lost wages

It is the whole foreclosed trajectory — promotions, raises, decades of contributions and pension accrual — not this year’s salary multiplied by the years remaining.

New York has noneNo cap on non-economic damages

Pain and suffering, disfigurement and loss of enjoyment of life are uncapped in New York personal injury cases. In catastrophic matters this element frequently exceeds the economic damages.

Special needs trust · MSABenefits eligibility must be protected

A settlement paid outright can disqualify you from Medicaid and needs-based benefits. Structures and special needs trusts exist to prevent that, and they must be designed before the money moves.

Stacking every available layerOne policy is rarely enough

We investigate umbrella policies, employer coverage, product manufacturers, contractors and municipal defendants to enlarge the pool of money that can actually be collected.

Wait for the medical pictureSettling early costs the most here

The full extent of a catastrophic injury often is not clear for a year or more. Resolving before it is can undervalue the case by millions and cannot be undone.

How we handle it

From your first call, in order.

Five stages. The sequence matters — evidence secured late is evidence lost.

Medical team assembly

We coordinate with treating physicians and independent specialists to document every dimension of the injury and its permanent consequences.

Life-care planning

A certified planner builds the lifetime cost projection — care hours, equipment, surgeries, home and vehicle modification — item by item.

Forensic economic analysis

An economist converts the care plan and the lost earning capacity into a present-value figure that will survive cross-examination.

Full liability investigation

We pursue every party whose negligence contributed — employers, manufacturers, property owners, municipalities — to reach every policy that can pay.

Settlement structure and trial readiness

We design the payout to protect benefits eligibility, and we prepare the case for trial regardless, because that is what moves the offer.
