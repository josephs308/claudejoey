What New York Clients Ask

New York is a no-fault state with $50,000 in mandatory PIP coverage. Your own insurance pays your medical bills regardless of fault. However, if you have a "serious injury" as defined by New York law — fracture, significant disfigurement, permanent limitation, 90/180-day disability — you can break through the no-fault threshold and sue for full damages including pain and suffering.

3 years for personal injury. 2.5 years for medical malpractice. 1.5 years for claims against New York City or other municipalities. Missing these deadlines permanently bars your recovery — no exceptions.

Labor Law 240 — the "Scaffold Law" — imposes absolute liability on property owners and general contractors when a worker is injured in a fall or by a falling object. It’s one of the most powerful worker protection statutes in the country. If you were hurt on a construction site in New York, you may have rights most other states don’t offer.
