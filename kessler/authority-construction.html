Why this case matters

What actually decides the value of this claim.

New York protects construction workers more strongly than any other state. Labor Law § 240 — the Scaffold Law — imposes absolute liability on property owners and general contractors when a worker is injured in a fall or by a falling object. Negligence is not an element. You prove the gravity-related accident happened and that adequate protection was not provided.

What you need to know

Read these six things before you talk to an adjuster.

Each one has changed the value of a real file. Most claimants learn them too late to use.

Labor Law § 240(1)Absolute liability for gravity accidents

Falls from height, falling objects, scaffold and ladder collapses. Owners and general contractors are liable regardless of care taken, and the protection cannot be delegated away by contract.

Labor Law § 241(6)Industrial Code violations create liability

Section 241(6) attaches liability when a specific, concrete Industrial Code provision is violated and contributes to the injury. We identify every applicable rule, not just the obvious one.

Owner · GC · subs · manufacturerMultiple defendants, pursued together

A single site accident usually implicates the property owner, the general contractor, several subcontractors and an equipment manufacturer. We file against all of them simultaneously to reach every available policy.

29 C.F.R. 1926OSHA findings are powerful evidence

Federal and state citations establish the safety standard and the breach of it. Marcus Webb’s investigative background is why we find violations other firms read past.

Third-party claimsWorkers’ comp is the floor, not the ceiling

Comp pays medical bills and partial wages and nothing for pain and suffering. Where a third party contributed, a separate personal injury action runs alongside it and is usually worth far more.

Full rights regardless of statusImmigration status is irrelevant

An undocumented worker injured on a New York construction site has the same right to compensation as anyone else. There is no exception and no reporting obligation.
