WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

Before you talk to the insurance company, read these facts. They can be worth everything.

New York Is a No-Fault State

Your own PIP insurance covers medical bills and lost wages up to $50,000 regardless of fault. But if your injury is serious — fracture, permanent limitation, or 90-day disability — you can break through and sue for full damages.

Pure Comparative Negligence

Even if you were partly at fault, you can still recover. New York uses pure comparative negligence — your award is reduced by your fault percentage, but never eliminated.

Rideshare Accidents Are Complex

Uber and Lyft carry $1 million in liability coverage when actively transporting a passenger. Getting to that coverage requires knowing exactly which policy applies — driver's personal or rideshare commercial.

Trucking Cases Are Worth More

Commercial carriers carry $750,000 to $5 million in liability. FMCSA violations — falsified logbooks, hours-of-service violations, drug testing failures — are powerful evidence.

Statute of Limitations: 3 Years

New York gives you 3 years from the date of the accident to file. Claims against government vehicles: 90-day Notice of Claim, 1 year 90 days to file.

First Offers Are Almost Never Right

Insurance companies open with low offers before you know the full extent of your injuries. Once you sign a release, the case is over forever. Do not sign anything without an attorney.
