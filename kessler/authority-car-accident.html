Why this case matters

What actually decides the value of this claim.

Vehicle collisions are the most common injury case in New York City and the most consistently underpriced. No-fault pays the first $50,000 of medical bills and lost wages. Everything beyond that — pain and suffering, future care, lost earning capacity — depends entirely on whether your injury satisfies Insurance Law § 5102(d). That single question is worth more than every other issue in the file combined.

We have tried vehicle cases in Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx and on Long Island. Daniel Kessler spent eight years defending these exact claims for carriers, which means he knows which threshold arguments survive a motion and which ones collapse.

What you need to know

Read these six things before you talk to an adjuster.

Each one has changed the value of a real file. Most claimants learn them too late to use.

Ins. Law § 5102(d)New York is a no-fault state

Your own PIP covers medical bills and 80% of lost wages to $50,000 regardless of fault. A fracture, permanent limitation, significant disfigurement, or 90 days of disability in the first 180 breaks you out of it and opens full damages.

Pure comparative negligencePartial fault does not end the claim

New York reduces your award by your share of fault and never eliminates it. Recovery is possible even at 99% fault — which is why adjusters inflate your percentage as a matter of routine.

Uber & Lyft — $1MRideshare coverage is layered

Uber and Lyft carry $1 million in liability while a driver is actively transporting a passenger. Whether that policy or the driver’s personal policy is primary turns on the app’s status at the moment of impact.

FMCSA — 49 C.F.R. 395Trucking files are worth more

Commercial carriers hold $750,000 to $5 million in liability. Falsified logbooks, hours-of-service violations and failed drug testing are documented, subpoenable, and devastating at trial.

CPLR § 214(5)Three years, and far less against the City

Three years from the collision for a private defendant. A city bus, sanitation truck or MTA vehicle requires a Notice of Claim within 90 days and suit within one year and 90 days.

Do not sign a releaseThe first offer arrives before you know your injury

Carriers open low and early, while the MRI is still unread. A signed release closes the claim permanently, whatever you learn about your spine six months later.

Car, Truck & Rideshare — what clients ask.

You claim against your own Uninsured Motorist coverage, which New York requires on every policy. We also examine resident-relative policies, any employer coverage if the driver was working, and umbrella policies you may not know you carry.

It depends on the driver’s app status at the moment of the crash. Actively transporting a passenger triggers the $1 million commercial policy; waiting for a request triggers a much smaller contingent layer. Establishing status is the first thing we do.

Your claim is usually stronger, not weaker. You reach the driver’s liability coverage, and in New York a pedestrian or cyclist struck by a vehicle is also entitled to no-fault benefits through that vehicle’s policy.

Clear liability with documented injuries: six to twelve months. Disputed liability or a threshold fight: one to three years. We give you an honest timeline at the start and update it when the facts change.
