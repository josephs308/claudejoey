Why this case matters

What actually decides the value of this claim.

When someone dies because of another party’s negligence, New York gives the estate and the surviving distributees the right to be compensated. Two claims run together: the wrongful death action, for what the family lost, and the survival action, for what your relative suffered between the injury and death. Firms that pursue only the first leave a substantial part of the recovery untouched.

We take the full legal weight of these cases so families can attend to everything else. That includes dealing with the carriers, who begin work within hours of a death and do not wait for a funeral.

What you need to know

Read these six things before you talk to an adjuster.

Each one has changed the value of a real file. Most claimants learn them too late to use.

EPTL § 5-4.1Who may bring the claim

The personal representative of the estate files on behalf of the distributees — a spouse, domestic partner and children first; parents and other distributees where none survive.

EPTL § 5-4.1Two years from the date of death

Not from the date of the injury. A claim against New York City, the MTA or another municipality still requires a Notice of Claim within 90 days of the death.

EPTL § 11-3.2(b)The survival action runs alongside

It recovers what the deceased could have recovered, including conscious pain and suffering before death. It is a separate and often substantial element.

Pecuniary injuryWhat the family can recover

Financial support the deceased would have provided, the value of lost household services, lost parental guidance for minor children, medical and funeral expenses, and interest from the date of death.

Joint and several liabilityUsually more than one defendant

In a vehicle death: the driver, their employer, a manufacturer, a municipality. In a workplace death: the contractor, the owner, the equipment maker. We pursue them together.

Before the funeralInsurers move within hours

Carriers dispatch investigators and adjusters immediately, and they are gathering statements while your family is still making arrangements. Representation should move just as fast.
